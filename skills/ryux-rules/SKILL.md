# ryux-rules (core)

> ryux.design design rules, version 0.2.0, MIT licensed.
> Apply to UI or copy work before considering it done.

## Evidence and honesty

- **RX-C-01** Every design decision references at least one real screen_id as evidence.
- **RX-C-03** No numbers or statistics without a real source; empty is better than made up.
- **RX-C-04** No invented testimonials, names, or photos of people.
- **RX-C-05** Mark placeholders clearly ([REAL DATA], [LOGO]); never disguise them as final.
- **RX-C-09** Designer notes are written by a human, not generated by AI.

Installed concern skills: `ryux-ui`, `ryux-copy`, `ryux-a11y`, `ryux-ux`, `ryux-local`, `ryux-code`. Load the ones relevant to your task.

## Delivery Gate

Design decisions must carry evidence (`screen_id`). Required rules cannot be broken; recommendations
only with a written reason. Reference data and structured review come from the ryux MCP
(`search_screens`, `heuristic_eval`, `delivery_gate`). Connect: `claude mcp add --transport http ryux https://mcp.ryux.design/mcp`
