# Motion in the reference (a ride-hailing booking flow)

What each moment does. The frames are in the run folder's ref/; they are not published here (another company's
brand).

- **Touch feedback:** a pressed button dims while the finger is down; every tap answers at once.
- **Scrolling:** the home page follows the finger 1:1, keeps momentum, and rubber-bands at the ends.
- **Shared element:** the tapped service tile grows into the next screen, and its illustrated rider leaves the
  tile and settles in the new header, in the same spring.
- **Pin drop:** the pickup pin falls in, its shadow sharpens as it nears the map, it lands with two small bounces
  and two ripples, then its label springs out of it. Confirming makes it hop and pulse.
- **Loading on the button:** the illustrated rider crosses the button while booking.
- **Searching:** the map zooms out around you, a radar sweeps, nearby drivers pop up one by one, then the camera
  zooms in on the one who accepted.
- **Same sheet, new state:** when the state changes, the card that did not change stays still; the title and
  subtitle push up from below, the button's fill animates to its new style, and only its label swaps. One snappy
  spring drives all of it.
- **Numbers:** a changing time or count rolls only the digits that changed.
- **On the way:** the driver rides the route, the route shortens behind a chevron trail, the illustrated driver
  leaps from the map into the card and gives a thumbs-up; on arrival the pin pulses and the plate flashes (the
  thing to match at the kerb).
- **Springs:** snappy and critically damped for pushes and sheets; a little bounce only for playful moments.
