Design the laundry order flow for Lipatan, an Indonesian laundry pickup and delivery app, mobile first (390 wide). The customer chooses a service, sets a pickup address and time, and places the order; then they follow the order until the clean laundry comes back. Show what the customer sees when a pickup cannot happen as planned, and when the weighed laundry differs from what they expected. App copy in Bahasa Indonesia; amounts in Rupiah. Look: fresh and friendly, with illustrated touches. Design it in Figma on a new page named "Porto A1" in this file: https://www.figma.com/design/FZmN4BAMmfNh7XMgVFjhHx/Porto-A?node-id=0-1
