# Questions asked before designing

The agent's reply to the prompt, verbatim. It was answered from the experiment's fact sheet (see the experiment README).

---

I haven't started the design yet. I need answers to three questions first, because each one changes a major decision.

1. **Market and currency:** Which market is the app for? For example Indonesia (Rp, QRIS, virtual accounts), the US (USD, cards), or somewhere else. Should the interface copy be in English or the local language?
2. **Product and payment type:** What kind of fintech app is it, and what did the user just pay for? For example a P2P transfer to another person, a QRIS or merchant payment, a bill or top-up purchase, or a bank transfer. Which payment method did they use (wallet balance, bank account, card, paylater)?
3. **Brand, statuses and actions:** Is there an existing brand or design system (colors, type, app name) I should follow? Besides "Success", which statuses must this page show (pending or processing, failed, refunded)? Which actions does the product really support here, such as share receipt, download, report a problem, or repeat payment?

Nothing has been made yet: no frame, no PNG and no reasoning.md. Once I have the answers, I'll design the frame in pen.dev, export the PNG and write the reasoning file.
