# Questions asked before designing

The agent's reply to the prompt, verbatim. It was answered from the experiment's fact sheet (see the experiment README).

QUESTIONS (no design made yet; I'm waiting for these answers before designing):

1. Market and language: Which market is this app for, and what language should the screen use? This decides the currency and date formats, and which payment methods appear in the details (for example QRIS, virtual account or e-wallet in Indonesia, versus cards elsewhere).

2. Transaction type: What did the user just pay for? For example, a bank transfer to a person, a merchant or QR payment, a bill or top-up, or several of these on one shared template. This decides what goes at the top of the page and which fields the details need, such as a recipient account, a merchant name or a bill or token number.

3. Brand and actions: Is there an existing brand or design system to follow, such as an app name, colours or a DESIGN.md? And which actions does the product actually support after a payment, for example sharing or downloading a receipt, reporting a problem, or paying again? If none are specified, I'll leave them as placeholders rather than inventing features.
