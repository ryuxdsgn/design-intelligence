# Questions asked before designing

The agent's reply to the prompt, verbatim. It was answered from the experiment's fact sheet (see the experiment README).

---

Before I design, I have three questions. Each answer changes a major decision. Anything you leave open, I'll treat as a visible assumption.

1. **Market and language:** Which market is the app for, and what are its currency and UI language? For example, Indonesia with Rupiah and Bahasa Indonesia, or an English-language app with USD. This decides the money and date formats, which payment methods appear, and the reference fields (QRIS, virtual account, bank transfer and so on).

2. **Type of payment:** What kind of payment does this page confirm? For example, a transfer to a person or bank account, a merchant or QR payment, or a bill or top-up. The detail fields, the recipient block and the follow-up actions differ for each. Should it also cover pending and failed results, or only success?

3. **Brand and platform:** Is there an app name, brand colors or a design system I should follow, or should I design a neutral fintech style? Is the target a mobile app only, or mobile plus web?
