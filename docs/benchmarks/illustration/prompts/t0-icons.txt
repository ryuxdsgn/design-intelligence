Tidewater is an app for a small sailing club: members book boats, join crews, and check conditions before they go out.

Design six custom icons for it: book a boat, crew list, weather window, tide times, running late, and safety check. They sit in the tab bar and in list rows at 24 px, in light and dark mode.

Deliver one SVG file per icon in a folder named `art/`, built so a designer can open and edit them. When you are done, list the files.
