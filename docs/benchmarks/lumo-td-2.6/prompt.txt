Design the transaction detail screen of Lumo, a digital wallet, shown after the user pays. Mobile, 390 wide, in pen.dev.

Context:
- Currency: Indonesian Rupiah, written Rp48.000 (Rp, dot for thousands, no decimals).
- Payment methods: QRIS, bank transfer, card.
- Statuses: Completed, Processing, Failed.
- Fee: Rp1.000 for card payments; QRIS and bank transfer are free. Show the fee on its own line.
- Problems can be reported in the app up to 30 days after the payment.
- Brand: Lumo. Primary color teal #0F766E. Typeface Plus Jakarta Sans. Tone: friendly and calm.
- Sample transaction for the main screen: Rp48.000 paid at Kopi Kenangan Sudirman with a card ending 4821, 6 Oct 2026 14:32, transaction ID TX-8F3K29QD.
