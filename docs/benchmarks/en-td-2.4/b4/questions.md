# Questions asked before designing

The agent's first reply, verbatim. It was answered from the experiment's fact sheet (see the experiment README).

---

1. **What kind of payment is this, and in which market?** For example, paying a merchant by card or QR, sending money to a person, or paying a bill. Also tell me the country or currency. This decides which details lead the page (merchant, recipient or biller) and how amounts, dates and payment methods are formatted.

2. **What can users actually do after paying in your product?** For example: share or download a receipt, report a problem or dispute the charge, request a refund, pay again, or split the bill. I'll only show actions that exist, and the main one decides the primary button.

3. **Is there an existing brand or design system** (name, colors, font, iOS/Android or web)? If not, I'll design a neutral mobile screen at 390px wide with its own small set of tokens and label them as assumptions.
