# Questions asked before designing

The agent's first reply, verbatim. It was answered from the experiment's fact sheet (see the experiment README).

---

1. **Which market and currency is this for?** (For example Indonesia with IDR and Bahasa Indonesia, or the US with USD and English.) This decides the locale, the money and date formats, and which payment methods and reference fields appear on the page.

2. **What kind of payment is it, and how was it paid?** For example: paying a merchant by QR, sending money to another person or bank account, paying a bill or top-up, or a card purchase. Paid from the wallet balance, a linked bank, or a card? This decides what the page leads with (who was paid, and the fee breakdown) and which reference fields to show.

3. **Is there an existing brand or design system I should follow, and which actions does the product actually support after a payment?** For example: share or download a receipt, report a problem or contact support, pay again, split. If there isn't one, I'll use a neutral, restrained style and show only "Done" plus "Share receipt", and mark the rest as assumptions.
