# Questions asked before designing

The agent's first reply, verbatim. It was answered from the experiment's fact sheet (see the experiment README).

---

1. **Which kind of payment is this, and in which market?** For example: paying a merchant with a card or wallet, sending money to a person, or paying a bill, and the country and currency. This decides which fields the page shows (recipient or merchant, fee, reference number, local payment method) and how amounts and dates are written.

2. **After paying, what can the user actually do in your product?** For example: share or download a receipt, report a problem or dispute, request a refund, pay again, or contact support. I'll only design actions that really exist and won't invent refund or dispute rules.

3. **Is there an existing brand or design system I should follow (colors, type, components), and is this mobile only or also web?** The canvas only has unrelated audit and experiment frames, so I can't tell which design system this app uses.
