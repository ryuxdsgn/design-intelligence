# Questions asked before designing

The agent's first reply, verbatim. It was answered from the experiment's fact sheet (see the experiment README).

---

Before I design, three questions. Each answer changes a major part of the layout or content:

1. **What kind of payment is it?** For example, paying a merchant (QR or card), a transfer to another person or bank account, a bill or top-up, or more than one of these. This decides what the page leads with (the recipient or the merchant) and which details matter, such as reference numbers, account details, or an order or invoice.

2. **Which market and currency is the app for?** For example Indonesia in IDR, the US in USD, or the EU in EUR. This sets how money, dates and payment methods are written, and what users there expect to see on a receipt.

3. **What can the user actually do after paying, and are there brand guidelines?** For example: share or download a receipt, report a problem or request a refund, repeat the payment, or go back home. Should it also cover states other than success, such as pending or failed? If the app has brand colors, fonts or a design system, please share them. Otherwise I'll design a neutral, trustworthy look and list my assumptions.
