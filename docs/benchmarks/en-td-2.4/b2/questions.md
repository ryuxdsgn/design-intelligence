# Questions asked before designing

The agent's first reply, verbatim. It was answered from the experiment's fact sheet (see the experiment README).

---

Before I design, three questions. Each answer changes a major part of the screen:

1. **Market and language.** Which country is the app for, and what language should the UI use (for example Indonesia in Bahasa Indonesia with Rupiah, or another market)? This decides the currency and date format, and which payment details show up (QRIS, virtual account, card).

2. **What kind of payment is this?** Paying a merchant (in-store, QR or online), sending money to another person or bank account, or paying a bill or top-up? Each one needs different receipt details, such as the recipient's account versus the merchant and order reference.

3. **Brand and platform.** Is there an existing brand (app name, colours, type) I should follow, and is this a mobile app screen only, or also web? The canvas has no components or brand for this product. If there's no brand, I'll make my own restrained, neutral style and label it as an assumption.
