# Questions asked before designing

The agent's first reply, verbatim. It was answered from the experiment's fact sheet (see the experiment README).

---

1. What kind of payment is this page for: buying from a merchant (card or QR), sending money to a person, or paying a bill? It changes who appears at the top and which actions follow (receipt and dispute, versus "send again" or split).
2. Which market and currency is it for, and which payment methods does the app support (for example card, wallet balance, bank transfer, QR)? That decides how amounts, fees, reference numbers and dates are shown.
3. Is it a mobile app only, or web too? Is there a brand or design system I should follow (colours, type, components), or should I start from a neutral fintech style?
